Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds are handled for paid training programs offered by VeriForge AI Technologies.

1. Cancellation by You

  • 7 or more days before the batch start date: you are eligible for a full refund of the course fee.
  • Less than 7 days before, or after the batch has started: the fee is non-refundable, as your seat has been reserved and materials/resources allocated.

2. Cancellation by Us

If we cancel or reschedule a batch for any reason, you may choose to (a) transfer to the next available batch, or (b) receive a full refund of the amount paid.

3. How to Request a Refund

To request a cancellation or refund, email chiragkedia@gmail.com from your registered email address with your name, course and payment reference. Requests are acknowledged within 2 business days.

4. Refund Processing

  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method via Razorpay.
  • Refunds are typically credited within 5–7 business days of approval, subject to your bank's or payment provider's timelines.
  • Any payment gateway charges, where applicable, may be deducted from the refund amount.
