Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

VeriForge AI Technologies ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

2. How We Use Your Information

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information. Payments are handled over encrypted connections by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant provider.

We retain enrollment and transaction records for as long as necessary to provide our services and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

6. Your Rights
