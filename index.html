Our differentiator

Verification of IP / SS / SoC using AI

We embed artificial intelligence directly into the verification loop — turning weeks of manual effort into automated, data-driven insight.

  • Coverage-guided stimulus: ML models steer constrained-random tests toward unexercised states.
  • Intelligent bug hunting: anomaly detection surfaces corner-case failures earlier.
  • Regression optimization: AI ranks and prunes tests to cut compute cost and turnaround time.
  • Automated triage: failures are clustered and root-caused with LLM-assisted log analysis.
  • Predictive sign-off: forecast coverage closure and schedule risk before tape-out.
